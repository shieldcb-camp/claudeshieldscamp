Elite Camp

A one-day camp for top-tier North Carolina talent — competitive 5-on-5 runs, fundamentals, and a first-hand look at what a college practice is really like. Come join us!

Event Description

The staff at William Peace University (WPU) is proud to hold our annual Elite Camp on October 10, 2026. This is a great chance for prospective student-athletes to showcase their talents, display their work ethic, learn about the recruiting process, and get better acquainted with the staff at Peace.

Our camp is designed for top-tier players in the state of North Carolina. The session includes a full college practice, including a sneak peek into what a college film session looks like. Our up-tempo style of basketball is ingrained into every single drill we run — attendees will see first-hand what being a college basketball player is all about. Come join our program for a day!

We'll offer campus tours before camp begins — strongly recommended for anyone interested in attending Peace. Our campus sits right in the heart of the state's capital city, nestled next to The Village District and Seaboard Station — the only co-ed Division III program the Research Triangle has to offer.

Contact us if you're interested in:

  • Becoming a hooper > being a robot
  • Wanting to get your license to let it fly
  • Learning more about the college recruiting process
  • Experiencing the structure of a college practice
  • Competing to win against some of the best talent in the "Hoop State"
