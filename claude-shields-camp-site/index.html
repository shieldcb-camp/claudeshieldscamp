About the Camp

Coached by the people who live it every day

Claude Shields Basketball Camp is open to boys and girls ages 6–12. Every camper receives individual instruction by members of the William Peace University coaching staff and players.

We want every camper to have fun, get better, and work hard. Two of our core values guide the whole week: Fail Forward Fast and Compete with Joy. We teach the campers how to respond to failure, to focus on the things they can control and be grateful for the chance to compete and grow! Come join our program for the week!

What We're About

More Than Hoops.

Fail Forward Fast

We teach campers how to respond to failure — to shake off a missed shot or a turnover and get right back to competing. We move on to the next play!

Compete with Joy

We teach our campers to be grateful for the opportunity to compete. Something we say a lot in our program is that our worth is not tied to our performance. This is what we pass down to them each day!

Camp Location

Hermann Athletic Center

Located on the campus of William Peace University, nestled right in the heart of downtown Raleigh next to The Village District and Seaboard Station — Peace is the only co-ed Division III program the Research Triangle has to offer.

Every camper is individually instructed by members of the Peace coaching staff and players throughout the week, and gets a nickname from a coach on day one — a small tradition that helps every kid feel seen and connected.

What to Expect

While at camp, you will…

  • Learn from current college coaches and players
  • Compete in two games per day
  • Participate in free throw, 3-point, and "Go for It" contests for prizes
  • Take part in daily clinics on different aspects of the game
  • Have fun!
  • Have fun, work hard, make every possession count, and play to win
  • Receive 1-on-1 individual attention catered to your game
  • Have an awesome experience learning the game of basketball
  • Learn ways to take what you learned home for continued improvement
  • Compete in week-long challenges that put your new skills to the test
